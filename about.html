THE REAL JOURNEY
😓
2015 — THE BEGINNING
Started Trading. Started Losing.
Like most traders, Parthipan entered the market in 2015 with big hopes. He followed YouTube videos, used every indicator he could find — RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands — bought every popular course. But month after month, the account kept going down. Not because he was not smart. But because he had no system.
💸
2015–2018 — THE DARK YEARS
Loss After Loss. Year After Year.
For three years, he traded with emotions — sometimes greedy, sometimes fearful, always confused. He overtrade on good days and panicked on bad ones. He tried 6 different strategies, joined 4 paid groups, and still lost money consistently. The market felt like a trap he could not escape. Many people quit here. Parthipan did not.
🔍
2018–2020 — THE SEARCH
He Stopped Following. He Started Thinking.
Fed up with indicators and tips, Parthipan made one decision that changed everything — he deleted all his charts and started from zero. He spent two years studying the market's behaviour from a purely mathematical view. Not patterns. Not candles. Just the numbers the market was already giving him every day.
💡
2020 — THE BREAKTHROUGH
The Numerical System Was Born.
After months of testing, he cracked something most traders never find — a completely numerical approach to options buying. No trendlines. No indicators. No subjectivity. Just clean, repeatable mathematical logic that told him exactly when to enter, when to exit, and how much to risk. For the first time in 5 years, he stopped losing.
🚀
2021–NOW — GIANT TRADERS ACADEMY
Teaching What He Wished He Had Learned First.
Parthipan started teaching because he saw hundreds of Tamil traders making the same mistakes he made. He built Giant Traders Academy with one mission — give traders a clearer framework, structured learning, and disciplined risk management. Today, 500+ students across India have learned the numerical approach through Giant Traders Academy.
"I did not build this system to sell a course. I built it because I needed it to survive in the market. And if it saved me after 5 years of losses — it will work for you from day one."
Parthipan M, Founder — Giant Traders Academy

WHAT WE STAND FOR

The GTA Way of
Teaching Trading

Three principles that guide everything we teach — built from 8+ years of hard market lessons.

Numbers Over Feelings
