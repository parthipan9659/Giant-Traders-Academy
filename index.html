RULES, NOT REACTIONS

Numbers decide. You execute.

  1. Collect the day's raw numbersOpen, high, low and close. No chart screen needed.
  2. Run the formulaIt gives support, resistance and bias as numbers.
  3. Fix entry, stop-loss and targetAll three are set before the market opens.
  4. Choose the strike by ruleCall or put, expiry and strike follow written criteria.
  5. Cap the risk in rupeesMaximum loss per trade and per day is decided in advance.
  6. Execute the plan, nothing moreIf the number is hit, act. If not, wait. No improvising.
