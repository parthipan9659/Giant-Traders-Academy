WHAT A REAL RECORD SHOULD SHOW

Numbers With Context

If a performance record is published, these are the details that make it useful rather than promotional.

📅
DATE RANGE
Clear
State exactly when the record started and ended. Avoid presenting selected winning trades as a complete track record.
💰
CAPITAL
Declared
Show the capital assumption and explain whether the figures are before or after costs, taxes and other charges.
📊
TRADE DATA
Complete
Include winning and losing trades, not only the screenshots that look impressive.
🛡️
RISK
Visible
Risk per trade, stop-loss rules and drawdowns matter just as much as profit figures.
